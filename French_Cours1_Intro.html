Pourquoi ça marche comme çaPourquoi l'introduction est la chose la plus rentable que vous apprendrez

Every speaking exam, every first meeting and every class opens the same way. Learn one minute properly and you have the opening of every conversation for the rest of your life.

And notice what it is secretly teaching: être without an article (je suis indien), avoir for age (j'ai vingt-trois ans), and parler without an article (je parle français). Three of the most-tested rules in A1, hidden inside a self-introduction.

Women add an extra -e: Enchantée, étudiante, indienne. Men: Enchanté, étudiant, indien.

Pourquoi ça marche comme çaPourquoi « j'ai mangé » veut dire deux choses en anglais

English splits the past into I ate and I have eaten, and the choice is grammatical. French does not split it at all — j'ai mangé covers both.

So half the difficulty you expect simply is not there.

But French splits the past somewhere else instead: between passé composé (a finished event) and imparfait (a situation or habit). That is the real division, and it is the first genuine wall at A2.

Pourquoi ça marche comme çaPourquoi CET existe

Ce ends in a vowel sound. Ami begins with one. Ce ami is awkward to say, so French inserts a t: cet ami.

That is the entire reason. Not gender, not a separate rule to memorise — just a consonant borrowed to keep two vowels apart.

Compare English a → an before a vowel. Identical motive, identical solution, two different languages arriving at it independently.

Pourquoi ça marche comme çaPourquoi les accents ne sont pas décoratifs

An accent in French is a letter, not an ornament. It does one of three jobs:

· change the sound — é, è, ê
· separate two words — ou/où, sur/sûr, a/à
· mark a letter that died — the circumflex, almost always a lost s

And the accent that moves (je préfère / nous préférons) is a stress mark. French stresses the last pronounced syllable; when the ending goes silent, the stem becomes the stress and the vowel opens. The accent is simply writing down what your mouth was going to do anyway.

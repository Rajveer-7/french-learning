Pourquoi ça marche comme çaPourquoi les consignes du prof finissent toutes en -EZ

Écoutez ! Répétez ! Regardez ! — these are not special words. They are the ordinary vous form with the pronoun deleted.

· vous écoutez → Écoutez !
· vous répétez → Répétez !

That is the entire rule for forming commands with vous. Once you see it, you can build an imperative from any verb you know — and you stop treating classroom instructions as vocabulary to memorise.

Pourquoi ça marche comme çaPourquoi le futur se construit avec un verbe de mouvement

Je vais manger is literally "I go to eat". French uses motion through space as a metaphor for motion through time — and so does English ("I'm going to eat").

The mirror image is venir de: je viens de manger, "I come from eating" = I've just eaten. Time as a road you walk along, with aller pointing forward and venir de pointing back.

Both constructions park the second verb in the infinitive and conjugate only the small one. That pattern gives you three tenses for the price of one.

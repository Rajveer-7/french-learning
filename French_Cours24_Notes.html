Pourquoi ça marche comme çaPourquoi QUEL a quatre formes mais un seul son

Quel, quelle, quels, quelles are all pronounced kell. The four spellings exist because quel is an adjective, and French adjectives agree — even when the agreement is silent.

So this is a purely written distinction, and that is exactly why every exercise on it is written.

The one moment you can hear it: before a vowel, the plural liaises. Quels‿amis = kell-Zah-MEE. That [z] is the only audible trace of the plural in the whole phrase.

Pourquoi ça marche comme çaPourquoi ÊTRE est irrégulier (et pourquoi c'est normal)

Suis, es, est, sommes, êtes, sont — six forms, six different words. There is no pattern because there never was one.

The reason is frequency. A word used a hundred times a day is never tidied up by analogy; a rare word is. English does the same thing — be / am / is / are / was / were is just as chaotic, and for exactly the same reason.

So the irregularity is not French being difficult. It is a mark of how important the verb is.
